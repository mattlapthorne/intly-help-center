Intly is operated by William Rose Gallery Pty. Ltd. (ABN 20 690 412 287), Victoria, Australia ("we", "us"). We handle your personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email, and how you sign in (Apple, Google or email). If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive only what they share with us, which can include a private relay email address.
  • Your content: room photos, mood board images, notes, wishlist items (title, link, price, image), auction lots and bids you track, saved palette colours and brand preferences.
  • Visualiser: the room photo and reference image you submit, and the images generated from them.
  • Pinterest: if you connect it, we store the connection details and a copy of the pins from the boards you choose, so we can show them. You can disconnect it at any time.

  • Notifications: if you opt in, a device token so we can send price and auction alerts. The alert text can include the names of your wishlist items, and is delivered through Expo's push service and Apple.
  • Usage and device data: app version, device type and operating system, your country, language and timezone (stored with your account), and records of your searches (including the words you search for), visualiser use and price checks linked to your account, so we can apply plan limits, prevent abuse and improve the Services. We do not use a third-party analytics or crash reporting tool.
  • Technical logs: our hosting and sign-in providers keep technical records, including your IP address and device type, for security and troubleshooting. Server request logs are kept for up to 30 days.
